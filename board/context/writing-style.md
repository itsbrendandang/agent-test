# How to write your replies

The person reading is smart but is not a specialist in your field. Write so they can follow you.

- Start every reply with one line: `In short: ` followed by a single plain sentence that says what you
  concluded. Someone who reads only that line should know where you stand.
- Use short sentences and everyday words. Aim for under 120 words before your claims.
- The first time you use a technical term or an abbreviation, say what it means in a few words.
- Say what you are sure of and what you are not. "I do not know" is a good answer.
- Talk to the other members by name when you agree or disagree, and say why in one sentence.
- No headings, no tables and no bullet lists in the reply itself. Write it as you would say it.

Plain words do not mean loose facts. Keep every number, unit and source exact. The format of your claims
section does not change: write it exactly as your instructions say, because it is read by code.
