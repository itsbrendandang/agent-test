## 2. Proposed grouping into role specialities

Each of the 50 skills appears in exactly one group. Counts: 10 groups. Index-only skills contribute their name only; triggers are drawn from full-text skills in the group, so a group whose members are mostly index-only has thin triggers and should be reviewed against the real packages.

### G1. Method development, benchmarking and literature synthesis (4)
- Scope: build or improve a computational method against a frozen, leakage-audited evaluator; author skill packages; compare methods from the published record without running them.
- Skills: 52 methods-landscape-review, 63 phylo-create-skill, 96 tusoai, 97 tusoskill.
- Triggers (from the text):
  - "Compare X vs Y", "which aligner/method should I use", "benchmark ... methods", "what does the literature say about X" -> 52 (comparison or topic mode).
  - "create, improve, optimize, audit, or package a computational biology or biomedical method" -> 97; autonomous multi-machine optimization with a built evaluator -> 96.
  - Create or repair a skill package -> 63.
- Cross-cutting rules in the text: freeze the evaluator before optimization; never read hidden labels or exploit file names/order; 97 requires at least 50 substantive iterations by default; 96 requires at least two datasets by default.

### G2. Pipelines and workflow engineering (3)
- Scope: turn a finished analysis into a validated, packaged Nextflow pipeline; author nf-core-style components.
- Skills: 64 pipeline-from-analysis, 65 pipeline-hpc-validate (index only), 66 pipeline-module-author.
- Triggers: converting the session's notebook/analysis to Nextflow (64); "author one Nextflow component", nf-core module/subworkflow lookup and vendoring (66); HPC validation and submission staging (65, inferred only from other skills' references; its own text is not available).
- Environment facts in the text: build locally under `/workspace`, never on `/mnt/results` (S3-backed; chmod ignored).

### G3. Target evidence, druggability and safety (5)
- Scope: disease-to-target evidence, tractability, expression specificity and safety signals for drug-target decisions.
- Skills: 58 off-target-safety-pharmacology (index only), 60 open-targets, 62 pharmacovigilance-ae-signal-detection, 93 target-tractability-druggability, 95 tissue-expression-specificity (index only).
- Triggers: "prioritize targets for a disease", EFO/MONDO ID (60); "Is KRAS druggable? Which modality?", "small molecule vs antibody vs degrader" (93); "adverse-event signals for <drug> in FAERS", class-wide or target-anchored safety, labeled vs unlabeled triage (62).
- Caveats from the text: association scores are prioritization metrics, not causal proof (60); FAERS disproportionality is not risk (62).

### G4. Bulk omics, proteomics, microbiome and multi-omics (8)
- Scope: bulk-level processing and analysis from FASTQ or public datasets to differential results, regulators, integration and signature tests.
- Skills: 53 microbiome-analysis, 57 multi-omics-integration, 59 omics-dataset-retrieval, 72 proteomics-diff-exp, 77 rnaseq-fastq-to-counts, 85 signature-response-enrichment, 86 signature-reversal-lincs (index only), 98 upstream-regulator-analysis.
- Triggers: "all available/comprehensive" public datasets for a disease (59); FASTQ or SRR/GEO accession, "build a count matrix", "ReadsPerGene", "STAR", "salmon", "detect strandedness" (77); TMT/LFQ proteomics DE (72); 2+ omics layers on shared samples (57); DE results to find driving TFs (98); 16S two-group comparison (53); longitudinal bulk signature activity vs drug response (85).
- Chain stated in the text: 59 (find data) -> 77 (FASTQ to counts) -> DESeq2 DE skills -> enrichment.

### G5. Genomics, genetics and variants (4)
- Scope: genetic causality, variant calling, risk scores and rare-disease case work.
- Skills: 51 mendelian-randomization-twosamplemr, 68 polygenic-risk-score-prs-catalog (index only), 74 rare-disease-case-prioritization (index only), 99 variant-calling-from-sequencing (index only).
- Triggers: GWAS summary statistics for exposure and outcome, "test causal direction", OpenGWAS IDs (51). The other three have no text; route by name only.

### G6. Spatial and single-cell (9)
- Scope: scRNA-seq core processing, trajectories and velocity, scATAC/multiome, multimodal integration, spatial, and Census queries.
- Skills: 76 regvelo (index only), 79 scatac-multiome-analysis, 80 scrna-disease-drug-discovery, 81 scrna-trajectory-inference, 82 scrnaseq-scanpy-core-analysis, 83 scrnaseq-seurat-core-analysis, 87 single-cell-census-query, 88 single-cell-multimodal-integration, 90 spatial-transcriptomics (index only).
